# Career Intelligence Platform v2

`build-career` turns one canonical, evidence-backed career graph into a complete recruiting and portfolio package. It extends the verified V1 runtime; it does not replace or rewrite its source facts.

## Command

```bash
build-career --output artifacts/career-platform --target "Principal Engineer"
build-career --output artifacts/career-platform --verify-only
```

## Platform stones

1. Resume Master — canonical facts and five audience-specific résumé packages.
2. PSYSOC-X — explicit reader profiles, never covert inference.
3. Web Design Pro — responsive portfolio, architecture site, dark/light/system themes, print, mobile, and PWA assets.
4. Skill Intelligence — 75 curated seed nodes with aliases, related topics, seniority signals, and evidence requirements; the schema is extensible and does not claim a complete thousands-node ontology yet.
5. Career Resources — canonical artifact inventory, local file hashes, and declared connector surfaces.
6. Persona Council — 15 deterministic expert review passes that can block but cannot add facts.
7. Career Connectors — declared GitHub, LinkedIn, job-platform, cloud-file, deployment, and model integration policies; no live write is implied.
8. ATS Intelligence — transparent structural heuristics for Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, SuccessFactors, iCIMS, and Ashby.
9. Interview Intelligence — STAR stories, behavioral strategies, architecture talks, system-design topics, and promotion outlines.
10. Job Intelligence — ontology mapping, gap analysis, target evidence, and interview topics.
11. Portfolio Intelligence — case-study and architecture projections plus deterministic portfolio and deployment ZIPs.
12. Infinity Gauntlet — one transactional build, exact hashes, fail-closed verification, and one receipt.

## Boundaries

The platform performs zero network queries and zero external writes during a local build. It does not claim production deployment, vendor ATS acceptance, accessibility certification, a current salary estimate without live market evidence, recruiter response, or a hiring outcome.
