# Infinity Stone Kernel Constitution

The kernel governs every stone, upgrade, and gauntlet.

1. **Truth outranks style.** A stone may reorder or clarify evidence but cannot change its status.
2. **The base model remains distinct.** Stones are reversible runtime specializations, not weight mutations.
3. **Identity is versioned.** Every stone and upgrade has a stable ID, semantic version, source path, and receipt boundary.
4. **Human agency survives activation.** No stone may exploit fear, shame, grief, dependency, confusion, or hidden traits.
5. **Composition is explicit.** Compatibility, conflict, precedence, and combined value must be declared.
6. **Execution is not completion.** A generated artifact is not verified until its required tests and receipts pass.
7. **Rollback is mandatory.** Deactivation must restore the prior loadout without rewriting history.
8. **Unmeasured means unverified.** Confidence language cannot substitute for evaluation.
