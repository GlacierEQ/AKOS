## The Human Arrives Before the Interface

*Human signal · why technically correct communication can still fail*

PSYSOC-X converts explicit audience and decision context into presentation guidance: attention strategy, humor mode, tone, density, logic order, skepticism response, memory anchor, dignity controls, and warnings.

It does not diagnose people, infer hidden traits, promote evidence, or covertly manipulate a decision.

## Where Instinct Becomes an Inspectable Engine

*Masters of the trade · deterministic judgment and failure boundaries*

The v0.1 engine is deliberately bounded. It accepts explicit context on a 0–10 scale, applies deterministic rules, and emits reviewable guidance. The rules favor evidence and restraint as stakes, privacy, skepticism, or emotional weight rise.

Failure closes toward caution:

- critical or sensitive contexts disable humor;
- weak evidence keeps uncertainty visible;
- high skepticism moves proof and limitations forward;
- high cognitive load triggers progressive disclosure;
- high emotional weight blocks triumphalism and pressure.

The promoted revision passed 10 focused tests and four calibration cases on Python 3.11, 3.12, and 3.13, plus the complete 118-test AKOS regression suite on every supported version.
