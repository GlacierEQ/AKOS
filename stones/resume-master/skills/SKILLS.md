# Resume Master Skills

## 1. Resume Fact-Graph Reconciliation
Locate the newest canonical package first. Normalize identity, roles, dates, claims, metrics, artifacts, evidence states, limits, and source references into one graph. Preserve contradictions until resolved.

## 2. Claim-to-Source Binding
Every material statement receives a source class and evidence state. Repository presence, review, execution, CI, deployment, public availability, and business impact remain separate.

## 3. Evidence-State Normalization
Use explicit states such as `VERIFIED`, `RECORDED_TESTS`, `REVIEWED`, `BLOCKED`, `GENERATED`, `DEPLOYED`, and `UNVERIFIED`. Promotion must be monotonic and receipt-backed.

## 4. Role-Fit Projection
Select and order approved facts for recruiter, hiring-manager, technical-review, executive, and machine audiences. PSYSOC-X controls presentation calibration; Resume Master controls factual eligibility.

## 5. ATS-Safe Serialization
Produce canonical headings, plain-text bullets, parseable organization/title/date structures, normalized skill vocabulary, and no hidden text or layout-dependent meaning.

## 6. Human Resume Information Architecture
Lead with role clarity and strongest proof. Use progressive disclosure, bounded density, and explicit evidence/limit language. Keep the first screen decision-ready.

## 7. Artifact Rendering and Inspection
Render DOCX and PDF, inspect every page, verify page count, glyphs, tables, margins, links, and overflow. A source document is not release-ready merely because it opens.

## 8. Artifact Identity and Release Receipts
Compute SHA-256 from exact bytes, record size and format, package related outputs, and persist a receipt that distinguishes generated, verified, published, and production states.
